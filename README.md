## What I'm focusing on
Next semester, I will work on the **barren plateau problem** in the training of quantum neural networks, looking at ways to **leverage classical surrogates** to mitigate it [[1]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.11740). I am also interested in **quantum error correction**, especially in the connection between **machine learning** and **topological codes** to enhance decoding strategies [[2]]([[2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08148-8). 

## Other interests
Outside of my main interests, I enjoy learning about the mathematical formalism behind theoretical physics — from C*-algebras in quantum information theory to differential and Riemannian geometry in general relativity and cosmology. Given my interest in QEC, I also want to learn about algebraic topology.
