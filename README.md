## About me
I am a graduate student specializing in quantum technology, with a strong interest in exploring the connection between mathematics and physics, inclduing
  - quantum computing,
  - quantum error correction, and
  - (classical and quantum) machine learning.
I am currently based in Stockholm, pursuing a master's degree in Engineering Physics at KTH Royal Institute of Technology.

## What I'm focusing on
Next semester (August-December 2025), I will work on the **barren plateau problem** in the training of quantum neural networks, looking at ways to **leverage classical surrogates** to mitigate it [[1]](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.11740). I am also interested in **quantum error correction**, especially in the connection between **machine learning** and **topological codes** to enhance decoding strategies [[2]](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-024-08148-8). 

## Other interests
Outside of my main interests, I enjoy learning about the mathematical formalism behind theoretical physics — from C*-algebras in quantum information theory to differential and Riemannian geometry in general relativity and cosmology. Given my interest in QEC, I also want to learn about algebraic topology.
